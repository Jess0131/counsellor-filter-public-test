Before you start

  1. Open the version your facilitator asks you to use.
  2. Use it as if you were choosing a counsellor for yourself. Change your preferences, apply them, and look at the results.
  3. Say what you are thinking as you go. There are no wrong answers.
  4. Use Test home in the prototype header to return here. Use “Reset session” on a prototype page to start over without refreshing.

All counsellors and details are fictional. Nothing you do is saved or sent. Refreshing the page starts again. Works on desktop, tablet, and phone.
